SECOND BRAIN OS

Persistent memory. Real context. AI that knows your work, your people, and your priorities.

TL;DRFive layers, two daily processes, seven skills. A vault remembers what you decide and who matters. Scheduled tasks gather and synthesize. Interactive skills decide. The result: AI that actually has your context, with a daily motion that compounds across sessions.
1
Persistent Knowledge
What the system knows, and remembers between sessions
PARA Vault
Projects, Areas, Resources, Archive. Active work separated from reference. Every note backlinked. Grows with you.
Nothing is lost, everything is findable
Active Priorities
Manual strategic list. This Week / Ongoing / Watching. Authoritative. Morning Brief reads this first.
Strategic focus survives noisy days
Daily Notes
Morning Brief + Evening Wrap. Carry-forward loop: each evening feeds the next morning.
No day starts from zero
Memory System
People profiles, project context, working-style preferences. Makes Claude understand your org's language and shorthand.
Claude understands subtext
TASKS.md
Tactical next-actions. Maps up to Active Priorities. Bridges "what matters" and "what to do about it."
Decisions become tracked work
Why plain text. Markdown is the most AI-accessible format. The agent reads and writes files directly, with zero infrastructure between model and memory. No vendor lock-in. Works with or without AI.
2
Scheduled Backbone
Runs without you. Gathers, synthesizes, writes the daily note before you sit down.
Morning Brief default 6:45 AM
Reads Active Priorities → Calendar → Slack → Gmail (3-tier) → Drive → Notion → Asana. Writes two altitudes: Strategic Landscape (focus / stalled / noise) + Ops Sweep (respond / delegate / kill). Includes rotating Daily Challenge.
You sit down to a briefing, not an inbox
Evening Wrap default 5:05 PM
Reads today's daily note and TASKS.md. Tracks what got done, what slipped, what's still open. Writes Leverage Assessment, Carry Forward (tagged handoff), Tomorrow's One Thing, optional weekly Thread.
Honest accountability + clean handoff
The separation principle. Scheduled tasks gather and prepare. They never decide. They surface what needs deciding. The system works even when you don't open a session.
3
Interactive Skills
Human-in-the-loop. Decisions stay with you.
Personalize setup
One-time interview. Picks your motion (strategic / coordinator / executor) from a vignette. Generates Memory.md and CLAUDE.md, then fills in the variables in your scheduled-task templates.
The package becomes your own in 15 minutes
Triggers: "/personalize-second-brain" · "set up my second brain"
Session Context
Reads Morning Brief → presents state → routes to skills. Adds delta: what changed since the briefing.
60-second context resume, any time
Triggers: "catch me up" · "where was I" · "what's on my plate" · new session start
Tactical Tasks
TASKS.md ↔ Active Priorities. Flags orphaned tasks, unpopulated priorities, stale items. Extracts commitments post-meeting.
Intent becomes tracked execution
Triggers: "update tasks" · "what's overdue" · "prioritize my week" · post-meeting
Vault Capture
Quick capture, session wrap-up, or suggested. "That three-bucket model is worth a note. Want me to save it?"
Nothing valuable stays in a conversation
Triggers: "capture this" · "save to vault" · "what should I capture" · proactive when frameworks emerge
Vault Cleanup
Weekly: dedup person notes (fuzzy match), fix orphaned backlinks, standardize naming, archive stale meetings, reconcile priorities ↔ tasks.
The vault stays clean enough to trust
Triggers: "clean up the vault" · "find duplicates" · suggested weekly if 7+ days since last run
Sanity Check
Audits claims against vault + external data. Rates each: confirmed, plausible, stale, contradicted, or unvalidated.
The system challenges you
Triggers: "sanity check" · "am I wrong about" · proactive when assumptions are 2+ weeks stale
Thinking Partner
Mental-model engine. First principles, inversion, pre-mortem, second-order thinking. Each matched to the situation. Detects exploring vs. defending.
Structured reasoning when stakes are high
Triggers: "help me think through" · "what am I missing" · naming any model · appears stuck
Thinking Orchestrator pattern
Stakeholder-lens orchestration. Brings perspectives you wouldn't naturally apply. Anti-reinforcement guards. Documented in customization-guide; build your own using your stakeholders.
Pressure-tested from every angle
Status: reference pattern, not yet shipped. Extension point for personalization.
